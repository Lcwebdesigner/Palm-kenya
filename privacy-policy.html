Information collected

This static website does not currently provide account registration, online forms, or a donation checkout. If you contact the foundation using the WhatsApp link or telephone link, the information you share is handled by the relevant service and the foundation’s contact process.

No analytics or advertising tracking code has been intentionally added to this website. The hosting provider or third-party services may process technical data as part of delivering their services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the foundation using its published telephone/WhatsApp details. An official email address and formal privacy contact should be added when confirmed.

Updates

This policy should be updated if the website begins collecting enquiries, volunteer applications, donations, analytics or other personal information.
