Website information

Privacy policy.

A basic draft explaining this website’s current information practices.

Draft status: This page is a starting point and should be reviewed and approved by the foundation before launch.

Information collected

The membership portal collects applicants’ full names, phone numbers and uploaded National ID documents (JPG, PNG or PDF) so authorised foundation administrators can review membership applications. If an application is approved, the system creates a membership number and a one-time activation code so the applicant can set a password and access the member area. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored as plain text by this website. The foundation should confirm the lawful purpose, access controls, retention period and secure deletion process for all membership information before collecting real applications.

Cookies and analytics

No analytics or advertising tracking code has been intentionally added to this website. The hosting provider or third-party services may process technical data as part of delivering their services.

External services

This website links to WhatsApp and may load fonts and illustrative photographs from external providers. Those providers may process technical information when your browser connects to them.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the foundation using its published telephone/WhatsApp details. An official email address and formal privacy contact should be added when confirmed.

Updates

This policy must be reviewed and approved by the foundation before the membership portal goes live. It should identify the responsible data controller, explain the lawful basis and purpose for collecting identity documents, state how long documents are retained, explain how members can exercise their privacy rights, and provide an official privacy contact. Do not upload or collect real ID documents until these details and the foundation’s data-protection obligations have been confirmed.
