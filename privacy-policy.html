Privacy policy.

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information The Palm Foundation Kenya (“the Foundation”, “we”, “us”) collects through this website and the membership portal, why we collect it, who can see it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. We handle personal information in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Who is responsible for your information

The Foundation decides how and why your personal information is used, so we are the data controller. You can reach us by email at collinswayne717@gmail.com or on telephone/WhatsApp 0799 364 171. Please do not send identity documents or other sensitive information over WhatsApp or other public channels.

2. Information we collect

  • Membership application: your full names, phone number, the password you choose, and an image or PDF of your National ID document.
  • Membership record (if approved): your membership number, membership status, the date you joined, and a record of any status changes (for example a suspension and its reason).
  • Sign-in information: your account is created and secured by our authentication provider. We cannot read your password.
  • Administrative records: a log of actions taken by authorised administrators, such as approving or rejecting an application or viewing an ID document.
  • Messages: anything you choose to send us, for example on WhatsApp.
  • Technical data: our hosting and service providers may process your IP address and device information when you visit the site.

3. Why we use it

  • To check that an application is genuine and decide whether to approve it.
  • To manage membership, including your sign-in, status and contact details.
  • To contact you about your application or membership.
  • To keep the system secure and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We rely on your consent when you submit the application, and on our need to verify identity before granting membership. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Who can see your information

Only authorised Foundation administrators can view applications and member records. Your ID document is kept in private storage that is not open to the public. Administrators open it only through a short-lived secure link, and each time an ID document is opened it is recorded in the administrative log. We do not share your ID document with anyone outside the Foundation unless the law requires it.

5. Service providers we use

We use trusted providers to run the website. They handle information only to provide their service:

  • Supabase for the membership database, sign-in and private file storage.
  • GitHub Pages to host the website.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile (where enabled) to block automated spam on the application form.
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash for fonts and illustrative photographs.
  • WhatsApp when you choose to message us.

6. Storage outside Kenya

Some of these providers store or process information on servers outside Kenya. We use providers that apply recognised security standards, and we only transfer information where the Data Protection Act allows it.

7. How long we keep your information

  • Rejected applications: the ID document is deleted within 90 days of the decision.
  • Active members: we keep your details and ID document for as long as you remain a member.
  • Former members: if your membership ends or you ask us to delete your information, we delete your ID document and personal details unless the law requires us to keep them.
  • Administrative log: kept so we can show who did what, and removed or anonymised when no longer needed.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to be told how your information is used, to see the information we hold about you, to object to its use, and to ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date or no longer needed. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, email collinswayne717@gmail.com or call 0799 364 171 and we will respond within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya at www.odpc.go.ke.

9. How we protect your information

  • Connections to the site are encrypted.
  • Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are never stored as plain text.
  • ID documents are stored privately and opened only by authorised administrators through temporary links.
  • Administrator accounts are protected by sign-in, and important actions are logged.

No online system is completely risk-free, but we work to keep your information safe and will act promptly if a problem occurs.

10. Children

Membership is for adults who hold a National ID. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising or analytics tracking. Your browser stores a sign-in session when you log in so that you stay signed in, and our security provider may set technical cookies on the application form. These are needed for the service to work.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
